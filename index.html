Common Questions
Everything You
Need to Know
How long is the book? ▾

The Local Church is intentionally short — designed to be read in one or two sittings. It's long enough to shift perspective, short enough that a pastor can confidently hand it to anyone without wondering if they'll actually finish it.

What books are in the Next Steps Series? ▾

Four books: Fresh Start with God (for new believers), The Local Church (for getting planted), The Holy Spirit Today (understanding the Holy Spirit), and Worry Free Finances (biblical money principles). Each is designed to be a simple resource churches can use at key moments in someone's spiritual journey.

How does this work alongside Fresh Start with God? ▾

The natural pathway: someone makes a salvation decision → Fresh Start with God helps them begin their walk with God → The Local Church helps them understand why getting planted is their next step. Many churches give them together as a "new believer to planted" bundle, either at the same time or in sequence over a few weeks.

Can we get a bulk discount on larger orders? ▾

Yes. A case of 100 books is $350 ($3.50/book) — saving 42% compared to individual pricing. For multiple cases or larger orders, reach out at info@johnsiebeling.com and we'll help find the right option for your church.

Is there a digital or ebook version? ▾

Currently available as a physical book only. The physical book is intentionally designed to be something a pastor hands to a person — it's a tangible gift, not just a link.

Is there a sermon prep guide or Pastor's Kit? ▾

Yes. A free Pastor's Guide is available above — ministry use cases, a suggested pathway, and sample messaging. Full digital Pastor's Kits with sermon prep notes are available through the store for titles in the Next Steps Series.
